<p class="lead">I love road trips. Flying gets you there; driving shows you everything in between, and I am on the road whenever work lets me be.</p>

## Ottawa to Montreal, roof down

In May 2026 I spent a few days in Ottawa with a red Ford Mustang convertible. It is a beautiful car, and it would have been a waste to keep it in the city, so I drove it to Montreal.

In Montreal I climbed the steps of Saint Joseph's Oratory, the great green-domed basilica on the side of Mount Royal. Then I drove back with the roof down.

## British Columbia

From Calgary the mountains are close, and British Columbia starts on the other side of them. I keep going back. Creston and Fernie are among the towns I have stopped in. The photograph at the top of this page is from September 2026, with rain on the windscreen.

There is no lesson here. It is simply the thing I do when the laptop is closed: pick a direction and drive.
