<p class="lead">Most mornings start in the gym. The rest of my time off the clock tends to go to things that cannot be done alone.</p>

## Dragon boat racing

In June 2025 I raced at the Lethbridge Rotary Dragon Boat Festival on Henderson Lake. A dragon boat only moves well when every blade enters the water at the same moment, which makes it a very honest team sport: there is nowhere in the boat to hide, and no way for one strong paddler to win it alone.

## Paintball

One Saturday in May 2026 a large group of us spent the day at a paintball field. I went in camouflage coveralls and a mask. It is the opposite of a quiet weekend, and I recommend it.

## Football

And there is football, the kind played with the feet. Through the summer I play in the evenings, when the light is long.
