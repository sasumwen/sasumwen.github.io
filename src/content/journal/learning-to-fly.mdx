<p class="lead">I am working towards my private pilot's licence. It is not finished, and this page will grow as the logbook does.</p>

The photograph is my table in November 2026. On the left is *From the Ground Up*, the textbook that student pilots in Canada have studied from for decades. Under everything is an aeronautical chart. On the right is a navigation protractor, for plotting a course on that chart. In the middle is the pilot logbook, where every hour in the air is recorded.

I spend my working days asking how a system behaves when something goes wrong and who stays in charge when it does. Flying training asks the same questions, on paper, long before anyone lets you near a runway alone. That part feels familiar. The rest is new, which is the point.
