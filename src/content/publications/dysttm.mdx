## Why fuse structure and function

Structural MRI tells you how a region is shaped; resting-state fMRI tells you how it behaves over the course of a scan. Schizophrenia research has long reported signals in both, and models that look at one modality alone leave information on the table. DySTTM is designed to read the two together, and to be interpretable about which regions and which moments mattered.

DySTTM is the most accurate of the three models and has the best ROC-AUC. The 3D ResNet keeps a small lead on recall and F1, which means it catches slightly more of the true cases. On a dataset of 174 people from one site, that is a result to build on rather than one to deploy.
