## In plain language

Most brain-segmentation networks decide, voxel by voxel, which structure each point belongs to. That works well in the middle of a structure and badly at its edges, which is exactly where researchers look when they compare a patient group with controls. BoRefAttnNet makes the network pay attention to the boundary itself: at each scale of the decoder, a boundary attention module sharpens the edge signal and quietens the background, so the final mask follows anatomy rather than blur.

## What the paper covers

- The boundary attention module and where it sits in a 3D U-Net decoder.
- Training and evaluation on sMRI scans from the Centre for Biomedical Research Excellence (COBRE) dataset, pre-processed with FastSurfer.
- Comparison against conventional 3D U-Net baselines on subcortical structures implicated in schizophrenia: hippocampus, amygdala and basal ganglia.

The chapter is an extended abstract. It presents the architecture, and leaves data preparation, experiments and results to the [MSc thesis](/research/thesis/), which also covers the downstream classification work (DySTTM).
