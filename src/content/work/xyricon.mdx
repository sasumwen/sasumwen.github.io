## Why I started it

After two years of research on medical imaging, I wanted to work on AI where the failure modes are physical and immediate, where a system has to be right, explain itself, and leave a human in charge. Xyricon Technologies was founded in Calgary in 2025 to build that kind of system. I lead it as Founder &amp; Principal.

## What Xyricon does

- **Products.** Auron, a real-time AI co-pilot for emergency response. See the [Auron case study](/work/auron/).
- **Applied AI and cloud engineering.** Reliability-first AI systems, orchestration and infrastructure for organisations with life-critical or regulated workloads, described on [xyricon.com](https://xyricon.com).

## Principles

Reliability, traceability and human oversight are not features bolted on later; they are the design constraints. Xyricon publishes no proprietary architecture detail, and neither does this page.

## Milestones

- **February 2026.** Presented Auron on the main stage of the BBVA Innovation Expo 2026 at Platform Calgary, as one of seven Black founders showcased by the Black Business Ventures Association.
- **April 2026.** Brought Auron's edge-hardware challenge to the Calgary Innovation Coalition's Founder Action Table.
- **2025.** A conversation with the Lethbridge Police Service about AI in public safety that shaped the product's direction.
