## The problem

When a public-safety answering point is overwhelmed by a storm, a mass incident or a staffing gap, calls queue. Some callers wait in silence; dead air is the worst thing a person in an emergency can hear. Dispatchers cannot be cloned, but the first minute of a call can be supported.

## What Auron is designed to do

Auron is designed to answer overflow emergency calls when dispatchers are saturated, perform real-time triage at the edge, and hand a structured, prioritised picture to the human dispatcher. It supports dispatchers; it never replaces them. The design aligns with the direction of Next-Generation 9-1-1 systems.

## Where it is

Auron is a prototype in development; it is not yet deployed with any agency. It has been presented publicly twice: on the main stage of the **BBVA Innovation Expo 2026** at Platform Calgary, and at the **Calgary Innovation Coalition Founder Action Table**, where I brought the hardest open problem, doing real-time inference reliably on edge hardware in a dispatch environment, to a room of founders and operators.

## Why it matters to me

A conversation with the Chief of the Lethbridge Police Service about AI in public safety made the stakes concrete: the people who will rely on a system like this are the ones having the worst day of their lives. That is the standard the product is built against.
